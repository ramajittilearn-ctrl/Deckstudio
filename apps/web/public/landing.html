The workflow

From idea to a ready post, in one clear flow.

Deck Studio is being built around a simple daily rhythm. You can see what is being prepared, what needs review, and what is ready for its publishing slot.

01

Plan the day

Bring topics, captions, sources, and creative assets into a single content card. Keep a rolling view of the next seven days.

02

Review the exact version

Inspect the media and copy together. A change creates a new version, so approval stays tied to what you actually saw.

03

Publish with a guard

The planned publisher will check approval again before dispatch. If approval or access is missing, the post stays off the public feed.

Creative formats

Choose the shape that fits the story.

The roadmap supports images, carousels, and short videos. Each format uses the same review path, with a preview of the exact content proposed for publication.
